# Simon, himself.

### Small tools. Disproportionately annoying problems.

I work at **Cloudflare** and build AI-powered apps, OpenCode extensions, and things that make my next project slightly less complicated. Results vary on that last one.

Based in **Switzerland**. Most of the code lives at the edge. The scope occasionally lives in fantasyland.

## Useful things that escaped localhost

### [YT;DW](https://github.com/simonhimself/ytdw)
**Because “just watch this quick video” is rarely a quick video.**

Turns captioned YouTube videos into concise reading briefs. Keep the useful bits; reclaim the afternoon.

[Try it →](https://ytdw.ssteiner.workers.dev/)

### [OpenCode Panes](https://github.com/simonhimself/opencode-panes)
**A home for prototypes beyond `final-final-v7.html`.**

A visual library for browser artifacts created with OpenCode. Upload privately, keep versions, and share when you're ready for opinions.

### [OpenCode Session Mover](https://github.com/simonhimself/opencode-session-mover)
**Moving folders shouldn't require a farewell speech to your chat history.**

Move complete OpenCode session trees between projects or repair them after a folder rename, with offline backups and integrity checks.

### [The Displacement Index](https://github.com/simonhimself/displacement-index)
**The future is here. I'm checking the distribution.**

A macro dashboard exploring whether AI productivity gains are reaching the wider economy—or arriving with labor and credit stress attached.
